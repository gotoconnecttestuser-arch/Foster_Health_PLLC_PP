1. Information We Collect

When you opt in to receive SMS messages from AFC Urgent Care Naperville, we may collect:
- Your mobile phone number
- Your name

- Communication preferences
- Message interaction data such as delivery confirmations

2. How We Use SMS Messaging

AFC Urgent Care Naperville uses SMS messaging to communicate with patients regarding:
- Appointment scheduling and confirmations
- Appointment reminders
- Visit updates
- Follow-up communication related to care
- General customer service communications

SMS messages are conversational in nature and are intended to support patient care and clinic operations.

3. SMS Consent

By providing your mobile number and opting in, you consent to receive SMS messages from AFC Urgent Care Naperville. Consent is not a condition of receiving medical treatment or purchasing services.

Message frequency may vary depending on your interactions with the clinic.

Message and data rates may apply based on your mobile carrier plan.

You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying:

After you send STOP, you may receive a confirmation message and no further SMS messages will be sent unless you opt in again.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Your data will not be transferred, shared, disclosed, or otherwise provided to any external organizations under any circumstances, except where strictly required by law.

We do not permit any external organization to use, access, or receive consumer data for its own purposes.

If we use service providers to support website operations, they are not authorized to receive consumer data as external organizations. Any limited access, where strictly necessary for operational support, is subject to strict access controls, confidentiality obligations, and a prohibition on any onward sharing or disclosure.

Access is limited only to what is strictly necessary to perform the specific support function.
Service providers must protect data through strict confidentiality and security requirements.
Service providers are prohibited from sharing, disclosing, or transferring data onward to any other party.
No broader transfer, sharing, or disclosure of consumer data is permitted except where strictly required by law.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. HIPAA and Healthcare Communications

As a healthcare provider, AFC Urgent Care Naperville may communicate certain health-related information via SMS. Patients should be aware that text messaging may not always be fully secure. Sensitive or urgent medical matters should be discussed directly with a healthcare professional by phone or in person.

If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 immediately.
